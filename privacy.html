Privacy Policy — Onchain Wire

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Onchain Wire ("the App") is a news reader that brings verified crypto and world news to users of the Pi Network ecosystem. This policy explains what information the App does and does not collect.

What the App collects

  • Pi account basics. When you sign in with Pi, the App receives your Pi username and user ID through Pi Authentication, only with the scopes you approve (username and payments). The App uses these solely to display your signed-in identity and to enable Pi payment features.
  • Payment records. If you make a voluntary support payment, the App processes the payment identifier and blockchain transaction ID through the Pi Platform API in order to complete the payment. The App does not receive or store your wallet passphrase or private keys — payments are confirmed by you inside the Pi wallet interface.
  • Server logs. The App's server keeps minimal technical logs (request time, endpoint, payment identifiers) for security and debugging. These logs are not sold or shared for advertising.

What the App does not collect

  • No passwords, passphrases, or private keys — ever.
  • No location data, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.
  • No advertising trackers and no sale of personal data to third parties.
  • Reading the news feed requires no account and no sign-in.

Third parties

Sign-in and payments are provided by the Pi Network platform and are subject to Pi Network's own terms and privacy policy. News content is produced by Onchain Wire from publicly reported stories by credited news outlets; links and source credits are shown with every story.

Data retention and your choices

You may stop using the App at any time. If you signed in, you can revoke the App's access from your Pi account settings. To ask what data associated with your username we hold, or to request its deletion, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
